1. Platform Overview
EduBook is a dedicated book management and distribution system for supplementary schools.
It digitises and controls the entire book lifecycle, from stock entry to final classroom handover, while coordinating administrators, teachers, and parents in a single system.
Primary Objective:
Ensure that:
•	Every required book is paid for
•	Every book handed out is recorded
•	Every stakeholder sees only what they need
________________________________________
2. User Roles & Access Model
EduBook is built around strict role-based access control.
2.1 System Roles
Role	Purpose
Admin	Full system control & financial oversight
Teacher	Book handover & class-level tracking
Parent	Payment & child-specific visibility
Each role has hard access boundaries to prevent data leakage or misuse.

3. Administrative Console (School Control Centre)
The Admin Console is the operational heart of EduBook.
________________________________________
3.1 Book Inventory & Catalogue Management
Feature: ISBN Barcode Scanning
What it does:
Admins can add books by scanning ISBN barcodes using a camera or scanner.
Business value:
•	Eliminates manual data entry errors
•	Speeds up inventory setup
•	Allows non-technical staff to manage stock
________________________________________
Feature: Automated Book Lookup
What it does:
After scanning, the system automatically retrieves:
•	Book title
•	Author
•	Standard identifiers
Business value:
•	Ensures consistent book data
•	Saves admin time
•	Prevents duplicate or misnamed entries
________________________________________
Feature: Real-Time Stock Tracking
What it does:
Every book movement is tracked:
•	Added
•	Reserved
•	Allocated
•	Handed out
Business value:
•	Prevents stock loss
•	Enables accurate reordering
•	Provides audit-ready records
________________________________________
3.2 Academic Structure Configuration
Feature: Localised Class & Level Setup
What it does:
Admins configure school-specific levels such as:
•	براعم
•	تمهيدي
•	Levels 1–6
•	Pre-GCSE / GCSE
Business value:
•	Matches real school structure
•	No forced “international school” templates
•	Easy parent understanding
________________________________________
Feature: Book Bundling (Book Levels)
What it does:
Admins group individual books into predefined bundles per level.
Business value:
•	Simplifies parent purchasing
•	Prevents missed or incorrect books
•	Standardises requirements per class
________________________________________
3.3 Student & Parent Management
Feature: Student Records
What it does:
Each student has a unique system profile linked to:
•	Class
•	Book requirements
•	Payment status
Business value:
•	Centralised student tracking
•	No paper lists
•	Easy year-to-year continuity
________________________________________
Feature: Secure Linking Codes
What it does:
Each student is issued a unique linking code that parents use to connect to their child.
Business value:
•	Prevents unauthorised access
•	No need to share personal student data
•	Simple onboarding for parents
________________________________________
3.4 Payment & Allocation Engine
Feature: Bank Transfer Payment Tracking
What it does:
Parents receive:
•	School bank details
•	A unique payment reference
Admins manually verify transfers and confirm payment.
Business value:
•	Works without card payments
•	Suitable for Sudanese & community schools
•	Clear financial trail
________________________________________
Feature: Payment Confirmation Workflow
What it does:
Once payment is confirmed:
•	Books are automatically reserved
•	Inventory updates instantly
Business value:
•	Prevents unpaid book distribution
•	Removes manual cross-checking
•	Protects school revenue
